01 / The argument

Why do people care this much about plantain?

Because the cooking method changes almost everything people notice first: sweetness, texture, how much oil is involved, and what the plantain pairs with.

Team fried says

Texture wins.

Ripe plantain caramelises and browns, giving crisp edges and a concentrated sweetness boiled plantain cannot reproduce.

Team boiled says

The meal matters.

Boiled plantain can stay more neutral and savoury, which makes it useful beside soups, stews and sauces where the plantain is supporting rather than dominating.
