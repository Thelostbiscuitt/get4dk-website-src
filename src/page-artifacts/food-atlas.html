01 / Separation

Why should location and cuisine stay separate?

Someone in Lagos may want Japanese food tonight, while someone elsewhere may want Nigerian food. Location should adapt availability and shopping advice—not decide what the user is allowed to want.

02 / Availability

How should 4DK decide what is realistically obtainable?

Use evidence and confidence. City context can narrow likely availability, but Pantry, receipts and observed shopping should outrank broad geographic assumptions.

Availability mode

Local-first

Local-first prefers easier-to-obtain substitutes. Open mode allows wider suggestions. Pantry-only uses what is already known.

Confidence model

Observed ≠ assumed.

A food item seen in Pantry or a receipt is stronger evidence than a generic city-level guess.
