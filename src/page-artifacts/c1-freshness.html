Will 4DK tell me food is spoiled?

Not from an estimate alone. Estimated freshness is treated as uncertainty, not a safety verdict.

Can it tell the difference between best-before and use-by dates?

That is the design. Date semantics remain distinct instead of being collapsed into one generic expiry field.

Does storage matter?

Yes. Fridge, freezer, pantry, opened, cooked and defrosted states can materially change the useful freshness context.
