How long does cooked jollof rice last in the fridge?

Direct answer

Treat cooked jollof rice as a refrigerated leftover: use it within 3–4 days if it was refrigerated promptly at 40°F / 4°C or colder. Refrigerate within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour above 90°F / 32°C. Reheat leftovers to 165°F / 74°C.

01 / State

What changes the storage answer?

Time in the fridge is only one signal. How quickly the rice was cooled, fridge temperature, handling and whether it sat out too long all matter.

Fresh.Recently cooked and cooled promptly.
Use soon.Still inside the recommended window, but meal planning should promote it.
Priority.Near the end of the 3–4 day refrigerated leftover window. Use or freeze.
Check.Storage history is uncertain. Ask or warn rather than fabricate certainty.
02 / Safety

What if the rice sat out before it went into the fridge?

If cooked rice or other perishable leftovers were at room temperature for more than 2 hours, or more than 1 hour above 90°F / 32°C, discard them.

Reheating is not a reset button. USDA notes that some bacteria can produce toxins that are not destroyed simply by reheating food left out too long.

Refrigerated promptly
Use the normal 3–4 day leftover guidance.
Left out too long
Do not save it by reheating. Discard it.
Storage history unknown
Do not invent a safe-until date. Treat the record as uncertain.
03 / Evidence

Why should 4DK store more than an expiry date?

Because “cooked Tuesday” and “safe until Saturday” are not the same fact. The system should preserve the evidence that produced an estimate.

Known fact
When it was cooked, when it was refrigerated and how it was stored.
Derived estimate
Estimated freshness, confidence and the evidence behind it.
User correction
Corrections should override an inference without deleting how the inference was made.
