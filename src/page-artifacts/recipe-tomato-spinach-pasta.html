20-minute tomato & spinach pasta.

A public 4DK recipe should be cookable without the app, structured enough for search, and still reveal what the kitchen system adds: pantry coverage, substitutions, scaling and remembered adaptations.

Ingredients

350 gPasta
2 tbspOlive oil
3 clovesGarlic
300 gCherry or chopped tomatoes
120 gFresh spinach
60 gHard cheese, optional
To tasteSalt, pepper, chilli flakes

Method

01

Boil the pasta.

Cook in well-salted water until just shy of your preferred doneness. Reserve about a cup of pasta water.

02

Build the tomato base.

Warm the oil, add garlic, then tomatoes. Cook until the tomatoes soften and release their juices.

03

Add spinach.

Wilt it into the tomato base. Do not keep cooking it after it has collapsed.

04

Finish together.

Add pasta and enough reserved pasta water to help the sauce coat the noodles.

05

Season.

Add pepper, chilli if wanted and cheese if available. Taste before adding more salt.

01 / Adaptation

What can I change without breaking the recipe?

Swap spinach for another quick-cooking green, use tinned tomatoes instead of fresh, or leave out cheese. The structure survives because the important jobs are starch + tomato base + green + emulsifying pasta water.

No spinach

Use another green.

Kale needs more time; soft herbs need much less. The function is “green vegetable,” but the timing still changes.

No fresh tomato

Use tinned.

Reduce it slightly longer so the sauce is not watery before the pasta goes in.

No cheese

Skip it.

The pasta water still helps the sauce cling. Cheese is a finish, not the structural reason the sauce works.

02 / Scaling

What changes when I cook for two instead of four?

Halve the ingredient quantities, but do not blindly halve pan size, pasta-water reserve or tasting. Cooking behaviour does not scale as neatly as arithmetic.

Arithmetic

Halve quantities.

350 g pasta becomes roughly 175 g. Do the same for tomatoes and spinach.

Pan behaviour

Keep enough surface.

A smaller batch can reduce faster, so watch the tomato base instead of using the same clock time.

Seasoning

Taste again.

Salt, chilli and cheese should be adjusted by taste rather than treated as perfectly linear.
