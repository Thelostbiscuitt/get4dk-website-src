03 / Questions

A few things to know before you start.

Can I use 4DK for free?

Yes. Guest is temporary; Free is an ongoing tier with real recurring limits.

Can paid AI usage grow without limit?

No. Paid plans have higher fair-use limits, and AI usage remains subject to service safeguards.

Does House merge everyone's preferences?

No. Kitchen state is shared, while people keep individual preferences and restrictions.

How many people can join a House?

A House starts with two people and can grow to six.
