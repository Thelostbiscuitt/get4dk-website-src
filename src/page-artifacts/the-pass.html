Culture / 001

Jollof isn't
just rice.

Everybody wants the final plate. Not enough people want to talk about what the dish is carrying with it. Jollof is taste, yes. It's also region, heat management, party memory, household method, and the kind of arguments food only gets when it actually matters.

This is where 4DK earns the editorial lane. We are not flattening dishes into generic recipe cards and pretending that's enough.
